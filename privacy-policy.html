Chorify Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 26, 2026

Publisher: Eva (Chenxi) Huang

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Chorify ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Chorify macOS application (the "App").

Information We Collect

  • Camera data: If you enable camera access, the App processes video frames locally to analyze pose and movement. We do not store or upload raw camera video.
  • Bluetooth data: If you connect a haptic device, the App uses Bluetooth to communicate with the device.
  • Usage data: The App may generate local session metrics (e.g., accuracy, timestamps) to provide feedback.
  • AI feedback requests: If you request AI feedback, the App sends performance metrics to our API proxy to generate feedback.

How We Use Information

  • Provide real-time dance analysis and feedback.
  • Enable optional haptic feedback via Bluetooth devices.
  • Generate AI-based coaching feedback (only if you request it).
  • Improve app reliability and performance.

AI Feedback and Third Parties

When you request AI feedback, the App sends performance metrics (not raw video) to our server proxy, which forwards the request to a third-party AI provider to generate feedback. The content you send is used only to fulfill your request.

Data Storage and Retention

  • By default, session metrics are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect or store raw camera video.
  • We retain any server logs only as needed for security and operational purposes.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used to analyze your movement.
  • Bluetooth: Used to connect to haptic devices.
  • Network: Used to access AI feedback services.

Your Choices

  • You can deny camera or Bluetooth access in System Settings.
  • You can choose not to use the AI feedback feature.
  • You can delete local session data by removing the app’s data folders.

Children’s Privacy

Chorify is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the effective date above.
